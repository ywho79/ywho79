# Ho Yah Wen

I am a firmware engineer with more than 24 years of experience in system firmware, platform initialization, BIOS, UEFI, EDK2, and open-source firmware development.

My career has spanned hands-on firmware development, platform enablement, debugging, architecture, technical leadership, and cross-functional engineering. ## Slim Bootloader

A significant part of my recent work has been focused on **[Slim Bootloader](https://github.com/slimbootloader/slimbootloader)**, an open-source, lightweight boot firmware solution optimized for fast and flexible platform initialization.

I have been involved in Slim Bootloader as an engineer, technical lead, speaker, and advocate for its adoption and development.

My interest goes beyond developing the firmware itself. I want to make Slim Bootloader more accessible to firmware engineers through technical articles, presentations, demonstrations, and practical explanations of how it works.
